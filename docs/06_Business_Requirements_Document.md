# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Project Name

AetherERP AI

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Introduction

AetherERP AI is a cloud-based Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) platform designed to streamline and automate business operations within organizations. The system integrates multiple business functions into a single application while leveraging Artificial Intelligence and Data Science to generate predictive insights and automate decision-making.

The platform aims to eliminate disconnected systems, reduce manual work, improve productivity, and provide business leaders with real-time information to support strategic decisions.

---

# 2. Business Problem

Many organizations struggle with:

- Manual record keeping
- Multiple disconnected software systems
- Duplicate data entry
- Poor communication between departments
- Lack of real-time reports
- Inventory shortages or overstocking
- Slow business decision-making
- Human errors in daily operations
- Limited business analytics
- No predictive capabilities

These issues reduce efficiency and increase operational costs.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

AetherERP AI provides a centralized ERP platform where all departments operate within one integrated system.

The platform will include:

- User Authentication
- Organization Management
- Employee Management
- Inventory Management
- Warehouse Management
- Supplier Management
- Customer Relationship Management
- Sales Management
- Purchase Management
- Finance Management
- Payroll
- Reporting
- Business Intelligence Dashboard
- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning Predictions
- REST APIs

---

# 4. Business Benefits

The proposed system will provide:

- Centralized business management
- Reduced manual work
- Increased productivity
- Improved collaboration
- Better inventory control
- Faster report generation
- Real-time business monitoring
- Intelligent business insights
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Better financial tracking
- Scalable cloud-based architecture

---

# 5. Business Goals

The project aims to:

- Digitize business operations
- Automate repetitive processes
- Improve operational efficiency
- Support organizational growth
- Enable data-driven decision making
- Provide predictive analytics
- Reduce operational costs
- Improve business transparency

---

# 6. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if:

- All ERP modules are fully integrated.
- Users can securely access the system.
- Organizations are isolated using multi-tenancy.
- Reports are generated accurately.
- AI predictions provide meaningful business insights.
- The platform is scalable and maintainable.
- REST APIs support external integrations.
- The application is production-ready.

---

# 7. Conclusion

AetherERP AI aims to become a modern enterprise platform that combines ERP functionality with Artificial Intelligence and Data Science to improve operational efficiency, business visibility, and strategic decision-making.
